## Design Context

### Users
Pack 170's registered adult volunteers use the CMS to keep public-facing pack information current. The login page is their secure, low-friction entry point to that work.

### Brand Personality
Warm, welcoming, and dependable. The page should make volunteer administration feel like part of the pack's familiar public presence while clearly signaling that this is a private workspace.

### Aesthetic Direction
Carry forward the public site's field-notebook character: warm paper, midnight blue ink, gold utility accents, and the Pack 170 mark. Use a light-only, calm composition with a clear reading order rather than generic dashboard styling.

### Design Principles
- Keep the sign-in task immediate and distraction-free.
- Use public-site visual language without exposing public-site navigation in the private workspace.
- Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, and readable error feedback.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid decorative animation that delays sign-in.
